About Us

A life spent giving
what little they had.

Mallam Yekeen Olaiya and Alhaja Afusat Bello lived with deep compassion for the less privileged. Though they were low-income earners themselves, their greatest joy was seeing children in school and families with access to good health.

The Yekeen & Afusat Bello Foundation was established to honor their memory and continue that work.

"
Because every child deserves quality life, quality education, and quality healthcare.

Mallam Yekeen Olaiya and Alhaja Afusat Bello understood hardship. As low-income earners, they knew what it meant to struggle for school fees and hospital bills. Yet, they never stopped giving, praying, and hoping for a better life for others.

This Foundation is our promise to them: that no child will be denied education because of poverty, and no family will suffer alone in health challenges.

We work to educate children and provide healthcare support to the less privileged across Nigeria.
